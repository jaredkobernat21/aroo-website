How it works

  1. Tell us about your yard. Your address, number of dogs, and anything we should know.
  2. Get your price. It depends on the number of dogs, and you’ll know it before we start.
  3. We clean up. You don’t need to be home. Waste is bagged and left in your trash bin, or hauled away. Your choice.
  4. Pay after the job. You get an invoice with a photo of the finished yard. Pay through Stripe, by check, or Venmo.
