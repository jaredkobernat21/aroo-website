Lawrence dog parks

Where your dog can run off leash in Lawrence, with locations, amenities, and the rules.

Updated November 2026 · By aroo, a locally owned dog poop cleanup service serving Lawrence and Topeka

The City of Lawrence runs two off-leash dog parks: Mutt Run in west Lawrence near Clinton Lake, and the off-leash area at Riverfront Park in North Lawrence. Here’s what to know before you go, based on Lawrence Parks & Recreation’s own page.

Mutt Run Off-Leash Dog Park

  • Where: 1330 East 902 Road, Lawrence, KS, near the Clinton Lake dam. Map
  • Amenities: Restrooms, a drinking fountain, water for dogs, and a small parking lot
  • The trails: Mowed paths run through open fields and along wooded areas. Signs along the trails mark where dogs can be off leash, so watch for them.
  • Cost: Free

Mutt Run is the pick for a long walk where your dog can roam. Expect mud after rain, and check for ticks when you get home (see ticks in Kansas).

Riverfront Park off-leash area

  • Where: At North Second Street and Highways 24/40 in North Lawrence. Map
  • Cost: Free

It’s the closer option if you live in North Lawrence or near downtown.

Hours

The city’s dog park page doesn’t list hours for either park. Some third-party listings say Mutt Run is open around the clock, but we couldn’t confirm that with the city, so check with Lawrence Parks & Recreation before an early or late visit.

The rules

These come straight from the City of Lawrence’s dog park rules:

  • Dogs must be on leash when entering and leaving the off-leash areas.
  • Dogs must be vaccinated and wear a visible tag.
  • No dogs under 4 months old and no female dogs in heat.
  • Dogs declared dangerous aren’t allowed.
  • Keep your dog in view and under your control at all times.
  • Handlers must be 16 or older. Kids under 16 need an adult with them.
  • No spiked or pinch collars.
  • Pick up your dog’s poop and toss it in the containers provided.
  • Fill any holes your dog digs.
  • At the first sign of aggression, leash your dog and leave.
  • You’re responsible for any injuries your dog causes.

Leash rules outside the dog parks

Everywhere else in Lawrence, your dog needs to be on a leash or inside a fence. Downtown, dogs on the sidewalk must always be leashed. See our Lawrence dog laws guide for the details.
