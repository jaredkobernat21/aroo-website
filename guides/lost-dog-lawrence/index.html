Lost dog in Lawrence?

What to do right now, who to call, and how long the shelter holds strays.

Updated November 2026 · By aroo, a locally owned dog poop cleanup service serving Lawrence and Topeka

If your dog is missing in Lawrence, act fast. The Lawrence Humane Society is the only animal shelter in Douglas County, and loose dogs picked up in Lawrence go there. Here’s what to do, based on the shelter’s lost and found guidance and the city code.

If you lost your dog

  1. Report your lost dog to the Lawrence Humane Society online or by phone at (785) 371-0473 (Pet Resource Center, daily 9 a.m. to 6 p.m.). They’ll call you if an animal matching your description comes in.
  2. Visit or call the shelter at 1805 E. 19th St. The main line is (785) 843-6835. The shelter also posts incoming strays on its Facebook page in real time.
  3. Call your microchip company and report your dog as lost. If you don’t know which company, the AAHA pet microchip lookup can tell you.
  4. Post on Nextdoor and local Facebook groups with a photo, a description (color, size, sex), and where your dog was last seen. Upload a photo to Petco Love Lost, which compares it to photos of found pets at shelters.
  5. Hang flyers at major intersections within about 3 miles of where your dog went missing. The shelter has a free flyer template and can help you make and print one.

How long strays are held

Under the Lawrence city code:

  • If your dog is microchipped or the shelter can find you, it will notify you and give you at least 5 business days to reclaim your dog.
  • If there’s no way to identify the owner, a dog that isn’t claimed within 3 full business days becomes the shelter’s property.

That’s why a microchip with your current phone number matters so much. Call the shelter for current reclaim fees and what to bring.

If you found a dog

  1. Check for a tag and knock on nearby doors. Lost dogs are often from the same neighborhood.
  2. Check the shelter’s lost pet listings and post a photo on Nextdoor, local Facebook groups, and Petco Love Lost.
  3. Get the dog scanned for a microchip at the Lawrence Humane Society or a vet office.
  4. To report a loose or captured animal in Lawrence, call Animal Control at (785) 832-7509. For an injured animal after hours, the city says to call the Lawrence Humane Society.

Prevent it next time

Tag and microchip

Lawrence requires a visible rabies tag whenever your dog is off your property, and an ID tag with your phone number gets a dog home fastest. A microchip is the backup if the collar comes off. See Lawrence dog laws.

Check your gate

Many escapes happen through a gate left open by a visitor or service worker. If anyone services your yard, make sure they close and latch it. aroo latches your gate after every visit.
