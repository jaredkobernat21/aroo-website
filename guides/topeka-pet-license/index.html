Topeka pet license guide

Who needs a license, what it costs, and how to get one without late fees.

Updated November 2026 · By aroo, a locally owned Topeka dog poop cleanup service

If you live inside Topeka city limits, your dog or cat needs a City of Topeka pet license once they’re 6 months old. Here’s how it works, based on the city’s official licensing page.

Who needs a license?

All dogs, cats, and miniature pot-bellied pigs 6 months or older living within Topeka city limits need a current rabies vaccination and a City of Topeka license, issued by the Topeka Police Department. If you live outside city limits (for example in Silver Lake or Tecumseh), check with your own city or Shawnee County.

How much does it cost?

Spayed or neutered

$10 per year for a dog, cat, or pig

Not spayed or neutered

$25 per year for a dog or cat

All miniature pigs inside city limits must be altered. If you see $20 and $8 on older websites, those are the previous fees. The city’s current page lists $25 and $10.

What you need

  1. The license application. Download it from the city’s pet licensing page.
  2. Proof of a current rabies vaccination that shows an expiration date. The city can’t issue a license if the vaccination has expired.
  3. Proof of spay or neuter, if you want the lower $10 fee.

Keep copies. The city doesn’t return paperwork.

How to get it

In person

Go to the Topeka Police Department, 320 S. Kansas Ave. (at 4th and Kansas), Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., except major holidays. Enter the police lobby through the south-facing doors, turn right, and look for the Animal Control window. Cash, check, money order, and major credit cards are accepted.

By mail

Mail the application, your documents, and a check or money order made out to “City of Topeka” to:

Topeka Animal Licensing
c/o Animal Control Unit
320 S Kansas Ave, Ste 100
Topeka, KS 66603-3640

Your tag is mailed to you once your payment and paperwork are approved.

Common questions

Is there a late fee?

Yes. A late fee of $2 per month starts 30 days after your license expires. Payment is due on or before the expiration date on your renewal notice.

What if my dog loses their tag?

A replacement tag costs $2, by mail or in person.

What happens if my pet isn’t licensed?

Unlicensed animals may be impounded, and owners may be fined under city ordinance.

Can I renew online?

The city’s licensing page lists mail and in-person options only. Check the page for updates.

Why license at all?

A license tag ties your pet to you, which helps them get home faster if they get out. It also shows their rabies vaccination is current. If your dog ever goes missing, see what to do if you lose your dog in Topeka.
