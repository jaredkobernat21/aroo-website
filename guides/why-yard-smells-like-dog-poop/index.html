Why does my yard smell like dog poop?

The usual causes, why it gets worse after rain, and how to fix it.

Updated November 2026 · By aroo, a locally owned Topeka dog poop cleanup service

The short answer

A yard that smells like dog poop almost always has more waste in it than you can see, plus urine soaking the same spots over and over. Heat and rain make both smell stronger. The fix is to remove the source, then rinse the problem areas.

Common causes

Hidden or old waste

Poop hides in tall grass, along fence lines, under bushes, and in corners dogs like to use. Even piles that look dried out still smell, especially once they get wet.

Rain brings it back

Rain rehydrates old waste and urine, which is why many people notice the smell most right after a storm. Runoff can also spread waste across the yard, and toward storm drains.

Summer heat

Warm temperatures speed up the breakdown that causes odor, and attract flies.

Urine spots

Dogs pee in the same few places. Urine is high in nitrogen, so those spots can smell like ammonia and turn the grass yellow or brown.

Hard surfaces

Patios, decks, gravel runs, and concrete pads hold onto urine and residue more than grass does.

How to get rid of the smell

  1. Do a full cleanup. Walk the whole yard in a grid, including fence lines and corners, and bag every pile.
  2. Rinse urine spots. Water the spots your dog uses most to dilute what’s there.
  3. Clean hard surfaces. Scrub patios, decks, and concrete with an enzyme cleaner made for pet urine, then rinse.
  4. Keep it from building up. Pick up at least once a week. See how often to pick up dog poop.
  5. Check your trash bin. Bagged waste sitting in a hot bin smells too. Tie bags tightly and keep the lid closed.

Common questions

Why does my yard smell like dog pee after it rains?

Rain re-wets dried urine in the soil and on hard surfaces, which releases the smell again. Rinsing those spots regularly in dry weather helps.

Will the smell go away on its own?

Not while waste keeps building up. Once the yard is clean and stays clean, the smell fades.

Is a smelly yard a health concern?

The smell itself is mostly a nuisance, but it usually means waste is building up, and the CDC notes that roundworm eggs in dog poop can survive in soil for months or even years.
