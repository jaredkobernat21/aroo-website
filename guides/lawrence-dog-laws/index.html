Lawrence dog laws

Rabies tags, leashes, poop, and pet limits in the Lawrence city code, in plain English.

Updated November 2026 · By aroo, a locally owned dog poop cleanup service serving Lawrence and Topeka

Lawrence’s dog rules live in Chapter III of the city code, updated in 2019. Here are the parts most dog owners need, in plain English. This is a summary, not legal advice, so check the full code if you need the exact wording.

Rabies shots and tags

Dogs and cats 4 months or older living in Lawrence must be vaccinated against rabies, and you need to keep proof. Whenever your dog is off your property, they must wear a collar or harness with a current rabies tag where it’s easy to see.

Unlike Topeka (see our Topeka pet license guide), the current Lawrence animal code doesn’t list a separate citywide pet license fee. The rabies vaccination and tag are what the code requires.

The leash law

Dogs can’t run at large in Lawrence. Your dog isn’t considered at large if they’re:

  • On a leash and under your control
  • Inside a building or a fenced area, with the owner’s permission
  • On your own property with a working electronic fence collar
  • Using a city off-leash dog park under its rules (see Lawrence dog parks)

Two details people miss: downtown, dogs on the sidewalk must always be leashed, even with an electronic collar. And a dog on someone else’s property without permission counts as at large, even if you’re right there.

Picking up poop

When you walk your dog on public property or anywhere that isn’t yours, you have to carry a way to pick up poop, like bags. And you have to pick up anything your dog leaves on public or private property, unless the property owner says it’s okay. People with a disability who are using a service dog are exempt.

The code doesn’t require cleaning your own yard on a schedule, but a clean yard is better for your dog, your family, and the neighbors. See how often to pick up dog poop.

How many dogs you can have

Lawrence limits each address to 4 dogs (and 4 cats) that are 12 weeks or older. To have more, you have to register with the City Clerk. Registration costs $25 and asks for each animal’s details, proof they’re current on rabies shots, your vet’s contact information, and your landlord’s information if you rent.

Selling or giving away puppies

Puppies can’t be sold or given away until they’re at least 8 weeks old and fully weaned, have had their first shots, have been dewormed, and are in good health, unless a licensed vet approves.

If your dog gets picked up

Loose dogs picked up in Lawrence go to the Lawrence Humane Society. Owners have a limited number of business days to reclaim them. See our lost dog in Lawrence guide for the hold times and what to do.
