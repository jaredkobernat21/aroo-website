Lost dog in Topeka?

What to do right now, who to call, and how the shelter’s stray hold works.

Updated November 2026 · By aroo, a locally owned Topeka dog poop cleanup service

If your dog is missing in Topeka, act fast. Helping Hands Humane Society (HHHS) holds stray animals for 3 full days before they move toward foster, rescue, or adoption. Here’s what to do, based on HHHS’s own lost and found guidance.

If you lost your dog

  1. Call HHHS right away at (785) 233-7325 and ask for Admissions Lost & Found. You can also submit a lost pet report online.
  2. Visit the shelter in person at 5720 SW 21st St. Admissions hours are Monday to Saturday, 11:30 a.m. to 6 p.m., and Sunday, 1 to 5 p.m. Staff can show you photos of incoming strays.
  3. Post a lost listing on Petco Love Lost, which can match photos of found pets, and in local lost and found Facebook groups and Nextdoor.
  4. Search your neighborhood. Walk and drive nearby streets, and ask neighbors to check garages and sheds.
  5. Check your microchip registration and make sure your phone number is current.

Getting your dog back

If your dog has been at HHHS less than 72 hours, there’s no fee to take them home. Bring a photo ID and proof of ownership, like vet records or photos of your dog. After 72 hours, standard reclaim fees apply.

If you found a dog

  1. Knock on nearby doors. Lost dogs are often from the same neighborhood.
  2. Post a photo with where you found them on Nextdoor, local Facebook groups, and Petco Love Lost.
  3. Get them scanned for a microchip at HHHS or a vet office.
  4. File a found report with HHHS online.
  5. If the dog isn’t safe to be loose, call Animal Control at (785) 368-9203 in Topeka or (785) 368-9300 elsewhere in Shawnee County.

If you can keep the dog for a day or two, HHHS says owners are often found within 1 to 3 days, and it may be able to help with a crate and supplies. Call (785) 233-7325.

Prevent it next time

ID tag and microchip

A collar tag with your phone number gets a dog home fastest, and a microchip is the backup if the collar comes off. Inside city limits, your dog also needs a City of Topeka license tag. See our Topeka pet license guide.

Check your gate

Many escapes happen through a gate left open by a visitor or service worker. If anyone services your yard, make sure they close and latch it. aroo latches your gate after every visit.
