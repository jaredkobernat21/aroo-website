Emergency vet in Lawrence

After-hours urgent care, the nearest 24-hour hospitals, and pet poison help.

Updated November 2026 · By aroo, a locally owned dog poop cleanup service serving Lawrence and Topeka

Lawrence has had few after-hours vet options, and many pet owners end up driving to Overland Park at night or on weekends. In an emergency, call before you drive so the clinic can get ready for you and confirm they can help. Save these numbers in your phone now.

Start with your regular vet

During business hours, call your own vet first. After hours, their phone line will usually tell you where to go. Several Lawrence clinics send after-hours emergencies to the 24-hour hospitals in Overland Park listed below.

Ad Astra Urgent Vet Care (new in Lawrence)

  • Where: 1837 Wakarusa Dr, Suite 3, Lawrence, KS. Map
  • Phone: (785) 856-0312
  • Posted hours: Monday to Friday, 4 p.m. to midnight. Saturday and Sunday, 11 a.m. to 8 p.m.
  • Status: Its website lists a soft opening on November 16, 2026, with limited hours at first.

This is an urgent care clinic for dogs and cats, and its website says it isn’t an emergency hospital. Think vomiting, diarrhea, or a cut that needs care tonight. For a life-threatening emergency, or when it’s closed, go to a 24-hour hospital.

Nearest 24-hour emergency hospitals

Both are in Overland Park, about 40 minutes east of Lawrence.

VEG ER for Pets, Overland Park

  • Where: 6347 W 119th St, Suite H2, Overland Park, KS 66209, in The Fountains shopping plaza
  • Phone: (913) 297-3676
  • Hours: Open 24/7, including holidays. Walk-ins are welcome, no appointment needed.

BluePearl Pet Hospital, Overland Park

  • Where: 11950 W 110th St, Overland Park, KS 66210
  • Phone: (913) 642-9563
  • Hours: 24-hour emergency care

Heading west instead? The Emergency Animal Clinic of Topeka is open evenings until midnight. See our Topeka emergency vet guide.

If your dog ate something toxic

Call your vet or an emergency clinic right away. The ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center is available 24/7 at (888) 426-4435. A consultation fee may apply.

Signs that can’t wait

Go straight to a 24-hour emergency hospital for:

  • Major bleeding
  • Trouble breathing
  • Collapse, confusion, or other changes in mental state
  • Signs of shock
  • Eating something toxic

Repeated vomiting or diarrhea, trouble urinating, a fever, or a wound that needs stitches are also urgent and worth a call.
