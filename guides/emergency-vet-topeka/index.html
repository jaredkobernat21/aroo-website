After-hours vet care, the nearest 24-hour hospital, and pet poison help.

In a pet emergency, call before you drive so the clinic can get ready for you and confirm they can help. Save these numbers in your phone now.

Emergency Animal Clinic of Topeka

  • Where: 5501 SW 29th St, Suite 1, Topeka, KS 66614. Map
  • Phone: (785) 272-2926
  • Hours: Monday to Friday, 5:30 p.m. to midnight. Saturday, noon to midnight. Sunday, 8 a.m. to midnight.

Some online directories list this clinic as open 24 hours, but its own website lists the hours above, closing at midnight. During the day on weekdays, call your regular vet first.

Nearest 24-hour hospital

  • K-State Veterinary Health Center, 1800 Denison Ave, Manhattan, KS 66506, about an hour west of Topeka
  • Hours: Open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week for emergency and critical care
  • Small animal desk: (785) 532-5690

If your dog ate something toxic

Call your vet or an emergency clinic right away. The ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center is available 24/7 at (888) 426-4435. A consultation fee may apply.

Signs that can’t wait

The Emergency Animal Clinic of Topeka treats these as top priority:

  • Major bleeding
  • Trouble breathing
  • Collapse, confusion, or other changes in mental state
  • Signs of shock
  • Eating something toxic

Repeated vomiting or diarrhea, trouble urinating, a fever, or a wound that needs stitches are also urgent and worth a call.
