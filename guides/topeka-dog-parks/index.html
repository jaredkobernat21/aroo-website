Topeka dog parks

Where your dog can run off leash in Topeka, with hours, amenities, and tips.

Updated November 2026 · By aroo, a locally owned Topeka dog poop cleanup service

Topeka has two public, fenced off-leash dog areas, both run by Shawnee County Parks + Recreation: Hill’s Bark Park at Gage Park and the off-leash dog area at Lake Shawnee. Here’s what to know before you go.

Hill’s Bark Park (Gage Park)

  • Where: SW 10th & Gage Blvd, Topeka, KS 66606, at the southeast corner of Gage Park. Map
  • Hours: 6 a.m. to 11 p.m., per Shawnee County Parks + Recreation
  • Cost: Free
  • Fenced: Yes, fully fenced with double-gated entrances
  • Small and large dogs: Separate areas for each
  • Amenities: Water stations for people and dogs, an open-air shelter, lighting for evening visits, benches and picnic tables, dog toy boxes, waste bag stations, trash cans, and a paved parking lot

Hill’s Pet Nutrition funded an expansion that doubled the park’s size. It’s the best pick if you have a small dog who’d rather not mix with the big ones, or if you want to go after work, since it’s lit at night.

Lake Shawnee off-leash dog area

  • Where: Inside Lake Shawnee park in southeast Topeka (66605). Map
  • Fenced: Yes. Dogs may be off leash inside the fenced area. Visitors mention picnic tables, shade, and a dog water fountain, and there’s one waste bag dispenser at the entrance, so bring extra bags.
  • Everywhere else at Lake Shawnee: Keep your dog leashed and pick up after them.
  • Hours: Shawnee County Parks doesn’t list separate hours for this area online. Call the parks office at (785) 251-6800 if you’re planning an early or late visit.

Lake Shawnee is a good choice if you want to pair dog-park time with a walk around the lake (on leash).

Leash rules outside the dog parks

The Topeka Police Department’s animal control officers enforce the city’s leash law, so plan on keeping your dog leashed everywhere except a designated off-leash area or your own fenced yard. See our Topeka pet license guide for the license and rabies tag your dog needs to wear.

Private dog yards you can rent

If your dog doesn’t do well around other dogs, apps like Sniffspot let you book a private yard or field near Topeka by the hour, so your dog can run off leash with no one else around.

Dog park tips

What should I bring?

A leash for the walk in, waste bags (stations can run out), water on hot days, and your dog’s license and rabies tags on their collar.

Is my dog ready for a dog park?

Dog parks work best for dogs that are fully vaccinated, come when called, and enjoy other dogs. Puppies should wait until their vet says their vaccine series is complete.

Why does picking up matter so much at a dog park?

The CDC notes that roundworm eggs from dog poop can survive in soil for months or even years, so one forgotten pile can affect the next dogs and kids who come through. Pick up right away, every time. The same goes for your own yard: see how often to pick up dog poop.
