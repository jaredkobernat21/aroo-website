Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

1. What we collect

When you request service on our website, we collect the details you enter: your first name, ZIP code, number of dogs, service type, your phone number and/or email, and any notes you add.

When you text, call, or email us, we receive your phone number or email address and the contents of your message.

2. How we use it

We use your information to respond to your request, schedule and provide service, send invoices, and answer your questions. We only contact you about your request or your service.

We don’t send marketing texts or emails unless you’ve separately agreed to receive them.

3. Where it’s stored and who can see it

Service requests are stored in a secure database run by our hosting provider, Supabase, and a copy is emailed to our business inbox through our email provider. Only Aroo can access them.

Card payments are processed by Stripe, and we don’t store your card details. If you pay by Venmo or check, we keep a record of the payment for our business records.

We don’t sell or rent your personal information.

4. Website analytics and spam protection

We don’t use advertising trackers or third-party analytics cookies. We count how many service requests are submitted and how many times our “text us” links are tapped, without any personal information.

To prevent spam, we use a scrambled, one-way version of your device’s IP address to limit repeated submissions. It can’t be used to identify you and is deleted within about a day.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we need it to provide service and for basic business records. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

6. Your choices

To see, correct, or delete the information we have about you, or to stop hearing from us, email hello@aroodog.com.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we’ll update this page and the date above.
